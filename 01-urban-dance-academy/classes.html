The four levels

BEG

Beginner

No dance background needed. Every step is broken down slowly; the first ten minutes are a warm-up and the basics of the routine.

BEG classes
INT

Intermediate

You know the basic grooves and can pick up an eight-count in a few run-throughs. The routine builds faster.

INT classes
ADV

Advanced

Full-speed choreography, more detail and texture. Best after a few months of regular classes.

ADV classes
OPEN

Open level

Everyone in one room. The routine is taught once, then each level gets its own version.

OPEN classes
