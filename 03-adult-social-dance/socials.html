The Friday social

8:30 to 11:30 PM, $12 at the door, free for anyone on a course. A 30-minute beginner lesson starts at 8. Come alone — most people do.

How a social works

Do I need to bring anyone?

No. Everyone asks everyone, and declining is fine.

Will I have to dance with strangers?

Only if you want to. Say no thanks and it's done.

What do I wear?

Whatever you'd wear to dinner, and shoes that turn.
