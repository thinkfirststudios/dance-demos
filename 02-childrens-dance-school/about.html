About Larkmoor

A recreational dance school in Fairhaven, run as a season from September to June.

Three studios and a printed fee schedule

Larkmoor teaches ballet, tap, jazz and acro to dancers aged 3 to 18. Classes are grouped by age and level, and the year ends in a single recital in June.
