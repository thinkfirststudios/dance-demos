How it works

Why parents choose Larkmoor

1

Placed by age and experience

New dancers start in the class for their age. Teachers move a dancer up or down in the first three weeks if it fits better.

2

Parents can watch

Every studio has a viewing window onto the waiting area, and the second week of October is watch week.

3

Costumes ordered once

One costume per routine, ordered in November. Sizes are taken in class in October.

The recital

Saturday June 12, Ellery Hall

Every class performs one routine. Four tickets per family at $18 each.
