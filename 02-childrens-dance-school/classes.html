The levels

Ages 3–6

Pre-ballet, creative movement, ballet & tap combo and acro 1. A parent waits in the lobby.

Ages 7–12

Ballet, tap, jazz and acro in levels 2 and 3. Most dancers take one or two classes.

Ages 13+

Level 4 ballet, jazz and acro 3. Older dancers can take up to four classes.
